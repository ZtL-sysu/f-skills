# Submission recovery and scoring control

Read before S1A and S6–S8. Existing-evidence checks, scientific/integrity reviews, journal lookup and safe manuscript revision are part of this pipeline's authorization; do not ask again just to switch a prescribed CCFA skill.

## Evidence repair

Classify a concern before stopping. Check supplied raw data, logs, tables, bibliography and alternative official citation sources first. Correct an evidenced typo or safely narrow an unsupported nonessential assertion with an explicit revision record. Never turn an unsupported central claim into more persuasive wording.

For missing experiments, write an experiment-return packet inside the run root: claim, defect, required discriminating test, verified dataset access, measured hardware/runtime feasibility, exact proposed output, owner, and unblock criterion. If the current user request already authorizes experiment repair, execute within that scope (using the research workflow when appropriate); otherwise finish the diagnostic packet and request the specific new experiment authority. Do not infer compute authorization from a submission-polish request. On return, reopen S1A or the earliest affected gate and invalidate its downstream suffix. Independent preparations may continue but cannot be recorded as passed downstream stages.

## Stable comparator evaluation

At S4 freeze the 10 comparator identities, accessible full-text/module sources, selection rationale and content hashes. At S6 freeze the rubric version and comparator scores with quoted evidence locations. Missing full-text evidence cannot be filled by inferred section scores. Keep fourth-highest comparator score as the existing operational threshold, not a statistically calibrated percentile or acceptance probability. Give score confidence and concrete defects separately.

At S7/S8 rescore changed modules against this frozen reference. Do not replace comparators or move their scores to get a pass. A justified comparator/rubric change reopens S4/S6 respectively and requires dependent rescoring. Log revision, resolved defect, unchanged facts and actual remaining issue. Same-agent role reviews must be labeled as such.

If a revision repeats wording changes without resolving the cited defect, diagnose the rubric/evidence problem before another rewrite. Continue useful evidence or prose repair within scope; when the missing condition requires new evidence or an author choice, return a concrete resumable blocker rather than inventing higher scores. Keep scientific gates above numeric presentation scores.

## Reuse and finalization

Bind accepted checks to input/renderer/rubric versions. Wording edits reopen affected modules and final PDF/package verification, not completed experiments. Global template, bibliography, metadata or numerical changes trigger all dependent checks. Preserve the original manuscript and retain the final clean-extraction build. No submission/upload is implied by internal repair authorization.
